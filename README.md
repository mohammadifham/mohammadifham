## `ABOUT.ME[]`

I am an **AI/ML Engineer, Software Engineer, and Full-Stack Developer**, and a Computer Science & Engineering graduate from Sambhram Institute of Technology, specializing in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning and Full-Stack Software Engineering.

My technical interests include building intelligent systems, developing scalable applications, engineering AI-powered products, and creating software solutions that address real-world challenges.

I have experience and working knowledge across Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Data Science, Generative AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), LangChain, Neural Networks, and AI model integration.

Alongside AI/ML engineering, I work with modern full-stack technologies, including React.js, Next.js, JavaScript, Node.js, Express.js, Python, REST APIs, MongoDB, SQL, and Tailwind CSS. I also explore cross-platform mobile application development using React Native and Android development technologies.

I have a strong foundation in Data Structures and Algorithms, Object-Oriented Programming, database management, system design, application architecture, and software engineering principles.

Beyond engineering, I am the **Founder & CEO of NexBuildOn Community**, where I lead the organisation's vision, strategy, growth, technology initiatives, products, platforms, and digital infrastructure.

I previously served as the **Technical Team Lead at OSCode SaIT Chapter (June 2025 – June 2026)**, contributing to technical initiatives, development projects, and the chapter's digital ecosystem.

Driven by a passion for technology and innovation, I focus on transforming ideas into intelligent, scalable, and impactful solutions.
